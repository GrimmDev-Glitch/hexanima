4x-UltraSharp
=============

Model: 4x-UltraSharp by Kim2091
Source: https://huggingface.co/Kim2091/UltraSharp
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes made for HexAnima: the original weights (4x-UltraSharp.safetensors, ESRGAN RRDBNet architecture) were
exported to ONNX (upscale/export_ultrasharp.py) and compiled, unchanged in content, into a 16-bit floating point
Qualcomm HTP context for a 256x256 input tile (upscale/build.sh, MODEL=ultrasharp). No retraining or fine-tuning.
