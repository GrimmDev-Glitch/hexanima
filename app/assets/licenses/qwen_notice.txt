- Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo transformer (GGUF Q5_K_S quantisation by Unsloth): re-packed into tiles for the Hexagon NPU (Q4_K tensors as a lossless 4-bit form, the others as 8-bit tiles); the weights are not otherwise changed.
- Lightning v2-RL rank-64 LoRA (LightX2V, Qwen Research License): applied for 4-step sampling; kept as separate low-rank rows next to every layer, not merged.
- Text encoder Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct (Apache License 2.0, Unsloth UD-Q4_K_XL quantisation) and its vision tower: re-packed for the NPU (4-bit and 8-bit tiles; the vision tower's 72-wide attention heads are zero-padded to 128).
- Qwen-Image-2.1 VAE: encoder and decoder re-written as 4-D graphs and compiled to fp16 Hexagon contexts.
- Tokenizer files converted to plain text lists.
