  Original: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B (GGUF quantisation by Unsloth: https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.5-9B-GGUF, file `Qwen3.5-9B-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf`)

  Author: Black Forest Labs
  License: Apache-2.0


## Changes made to the original works

- The Unsloth GGUF files were dequantised and re-packed into 32x32 tiles for the Hexagon matrix unit: 8-bit blocks with one fp16 scale per column and 32 weights, or (Q4_K tensors of the text encoder only) a lossless 4-bit affine form. K-quant groups of 32 are kept; Q5_K / Q6_K / Q8_0 / IQ4_XS tensors are stored as 8-bit blocks.
- The rapid-moss LoRA is not merged into the weights (its delta is smaller than bf16 rounding); its two matrices are stored next to every projection and applied as extra input tiles.
- The FLUX.2 small decoder was converted to a Qualcomm HTP (V69, fp16) context binary for fixed image sizes.
- The weights are not usable with any other software.

No other changes were made to the models. The licenses and copyright notices of the original works apply to these derivatives; see LICENSE.
